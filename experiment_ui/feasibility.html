What this can establish

A successful rollout establishes that the tested controller can balance that initial state under those limits. A high success rate estimates its recovery region for this sampled distribution.

Failure of rounded LQR does not prove discrete control impossible. Another discrete controller, such as predictive control or pulse-based control, could perform better. This study does not test swing-up from below.

Checks against false success

Both links must stay within 10° of vertical, with absolute angular velocities below 0.5 rad/s. The criterion is checked every physics step, not just when the controller updates.

Protocol and raw data

Linearize the exact Acrobot dynamics around [π, 0, 0, 0]. Compute a discrete-time LQR controller for each interval with Q = diag(10, 10, 1, 1), R = 1. Clip its requested torque to the selected limit. Three-level and nine-level controllers round that bounded command to evenly spaced levels.

All conditions share the same 128 initial random vectors, seed 30000. At 1×, joint angle errors are uniform within ±0.08 radians and joint velocities within ±0.1 rad/s. Scales multiply all four disturbances. Integration uses RK4 and the same Gymnasium velocity limits as the RL experiment. No reward is optimized or used to select a controller.

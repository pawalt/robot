Imitation, then reinforcement learning

Learning the whole controller.

Teachers provide training examples. At evaluation, a neural network alone chooses every torque from the current angles and velocities. No LQR handoff, trajectory lookup, or clock.

Success means balance throughout the final ten seconds. A fall does not end the episode. Kick tests inject random joint-velocity changes five seconds into a rollout; pre-kick balance is reported separately.

The three PPO follow-ups from eight-second imitation are complete. Early checkpoints improved approach control in two seeds, but the final policies regressed. Inspect selected and final rows, including the added earlier-start retention tests.

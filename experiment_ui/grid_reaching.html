What you’re watching

The mint robot moves toward the gold target. The trail marks visited cells. Each move costs −1; reaching the goal gives +10. A rollout stops at the goal or after 200 steps. Videos play six environment steps per second.

What the scores mean

Evaluation switches exploration off and tests every starting cell. Training includes random exploration, so its rewards can be lower. This is a fixed, empty grid; success here doesn’t imply general robotics ability.

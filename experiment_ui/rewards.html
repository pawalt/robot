Can reward shaping guide the approach?

Three rewards, three seeds, the same neural starting checkpoint. Every 45-second rollout batch starts equally from balance, six-second, eight-second, and twelve-second approaches. Rehearsal, network, compute, and PPO settings match across arms.

Baseline: height + balance bonus − global speed penalty. Braking: speed penalty fades near the bottom. Capture progress: braking reward plus potential-based progress toward the sampled neural recovery region. The recovery score is a local heuristic, not a guarantee from distant states.

“12 seconds” means starting twelve seconds before the end of a demonstrated trajectory, not a full swing-up in twelve seconds. Success means balancing for the final ten seconds. The controller is entirely neural.

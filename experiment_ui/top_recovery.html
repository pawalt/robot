Recovery after a release or kick at the top.

Try it yourself in the interactive playground →

These tests start near upright, or kick a controller that has already balanced. The original radial neural policies control every active step. No policy was retrained for these tests.

15° release: both links point 15° away from upright, initially motionless. Free fall: start 1° from upright, motor off for two seconds, then neural control on. Kick: swing up normally, verify balance, then add +4 rad/s to shoulder velocity and −4 rad/s to relative elbow velocity at 40 seconds. No state reset.

Policy 1 recovered from the tested top disturbances.

It passed all 288 release/drop episodes and both fixed kicks. The exact +15° release regained sustained balance at 28.4 seconds. After the larger kick at 40 seconds, balance returned at 76.2 seconds. Other policies have failures, shown below.
